DEPENDENCIES / NOTES
================================================================================
- Key packages: scran (findMarkers), edgeR, DESeq2 (pseudobulk),
  fgsea (GSEA), clusterProfiler (ORA)
- Pseudobulk DE is strongly preferred over single-cell DE for
  condition comparisons — aggregates counts per sample per cluster
  before testing, properly accounting for sample as the unit of replication
- Single-cell level DE (findMarkers) appropriate for cluster marker discovery
